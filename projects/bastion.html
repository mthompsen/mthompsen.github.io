  • Command-line detection engine: ingests Windows Sysmon/EVTX and Linux auth/syslog, evaluates them in-process against the full public SigmaHQ ruleset, maps every hit to MITRE ATT&CK.
  • Custom Sigma grammar evaluator (field modifiers, base64/windash/UTF-16 transforms, N of conditions); 2,479 of 2,480 pinned Windows rules evaluable, the 1 unsupported rule disclosed in every report.
  • Reports honest coverage: 314 of 706 enterprise techniques covered by the pinned ruleset, with blind spots split into out-of-scope, unread-telemetry, and writable gaps.
  • Outputs terminal table, JSON, SARIF, Markdown, and a self-contained HTML dashboard; ships a hand-written 8-rule local pack for techniques SigmaHQ misses; bastion bench measures real detection on labelled captures.
